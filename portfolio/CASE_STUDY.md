# Case Study — Prescriptive Analytics

## What this portfolio proves
Predictive analytics estimates what may happen. Optimization addresses a different question: **given objectives, constraints and available choices, what should the organization do?**

Across the course portfolio, business decisions were formalized mathematically and solved using spreadsheet and Python optimization tools.

## Facility location
Binary decisions determine which sites to open while coverage constraints ensure customers/cities are served. This demonstrates logical constraints, set covering/facility location and trade-offs between network size and service requirements.

## Workforce
Integer decisions determine staffing levels while coverage constraints ensure labor requirements are met at minimum cost.

## Transportation
Flow variables allocate volume through a network while satisfying supply/demand and minimizing distribution cost.

## Pricing
Nonlinear decision models trade price against demand/revenue and require a different solution method from linear programs.

## Why it matters
The strongest evidence is not memorizing Solver or Gurobi syntax; it is translating an ambiguous operating decision into a valid objective, constraints, variable domains and a recommendation that can be explained to management.
